How our queries operate

Returns 100% of results

We don't truncate. Every video that responds to your query — across ~35M channels and ~5B videos — is returned, with no volume cap.

Daily or weekly cadence

Daily pulls yesterday's activity at a higher rate. Weekly pulls the past week's activity at a lower rate. Both deliver in increments that power timeline charts: yesterday, week, month, quarter, year, past 18 months.

Owned media + sponsorships removed

We auto-strip anything published by the brand itself (across all their channels), plus sponsorship, licensing, and incidental mentions — so your earned-media signal stays clean.

All Signal, No Noise guarantee

Adjusted and Contextual queries are guaranteed. We tune the search strings until the firehose returns exactly what you'd expect — no more, no less.

Contextual is for generic-name brands

Orange Telecom, Dodge Cars, Specialized Bicycles, Columbia Sportswear, Liberty Financial — only Contextual Queries (1,000–10,000 terms per string) work for brands whose names overlap with everyday English.
